Accepted for review2026-10-13 following PR6 human progression failure and explicit
operator direction to replace prototype art. Content13 changes draft traces and
boss behavior intentionally; worldgen2 geometry and save-schema2 stay compatible.

A mandatory combination cannot rely on omitted seeded modules or random offers.
Reserve universal Fire/Tools inside existing selection count and one eligible
non-reserved level-draft slot before Industrial. Preserve choice, effects and
origin diversity; no free completion. Derive UI advice from the canonical route.

generated PNGs are retained, normalized and hashed separately, review-pending;
never call static poses complete production sequences. New deterministic boss
state owns warnings/locked aims/attacks/recovery and participates in snapshots.
Cosmetic floor remains walkable and cannot alter world geometry or RNG.

No dependency. Existing performance thresholds, real failed human evidence and
main history remain intact. See QUALITY_CLOSURE_REVIEW.md for proofs/limits.
