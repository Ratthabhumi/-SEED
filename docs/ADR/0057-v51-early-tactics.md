Owner directive V5.1; base18ec2b5 / PR41. Historical Combat1/5, Upgrade2/5 and negative C26 Natural36 results remain evidence, not design targets. V48's mixed spatial observations do not justify density/HP/Boss retuning here.

## Decision

Add four voluntary Stone Tech branches on the existing domain foundations: Split volley (+1 Kinetic projectile), First arc (Energy chain), Layered traps (+1 mine), Outer ward (+1 Defense orbit blade). Reuse canonical effects/emission/collision/render paths. Keep all eight old numerical application IDs and effects for history compatibility; no stat or permanent currency is granted at spawn.

For World1 Stone level drafts before two actual Tech choices, protect the highest already-sampled legal attack-pattern candidate in slot0. Consume the same Gumbel draw per candidate and the same three quality draws; do not resample for a preferred result. Already-unlocked chain is not a new protected opportunity. Existing recipe, reserve, POI-domain and reroll-novelty contracts remain authoritative; the player may decline, reserve, reroll or skip. This guarantees an initial opportunity, not a forced pick/build or every post-reroll combination of constraints.

Content27 records the replay change. Worldgen3 and Save3 stay pinned. Child world laws remain18, while new Tech content is available in child graphs too; the protected opening policy itself is World1-only. Old run traces are not expected to replay across Content versions. Save3 archive/Insight/cosmetics and old application meanings remain intact.

The initial pool intentionally adds two legal branches per Origin. Preserve the old ≤9 non-tactical pool budget, assert exactly one added branch per active family, and keep the actual draft hand at three. The old whole-pool≤9 assertion predates this authorized content design; do not remove old stat choices or change runtime prerequisites just to satisfy that obsolete assumption. Native performance/CI thresholds are unchanged.

## World2 interaction

Reuse actual scarce-knowledge/research bonuses and the existing raid scheduler. A read-only decision query projects the exact current multiplier and the result of a legal tier-one Research specialization at a real neutral owned site. It reports only a live raid on an actual owned base. The optional picker offers canonical specialization or deferring research while tracking that threatened base. Tracking moves no actors, grants no rewards and changes no simulation state. No new director, child law, fake site or attack is added.

At720p a captured initial layout hid the defense action below the fold (RED viewport ratio0). Use a wider, responsive choice grid and shared footer only on this scarcity picker; preserve large-text scrolling and keyboard reachability. New tactical cards explain the actual behaviors using existing UI/assets/tokens. Actor scales/hitboxes, Boss behavior, terrain/materials, native renderer/review contracts and performance thresholds remain unchanged.
