# Roadmap

## V0.1 — Vertical Slice (current)

Playable deterministic run: 6 ages, seeded Tech DAG, 4 weapon families, 4 enemy
lineages, threat director, boss, endless ascension, TH/EN, itch ZIP.

## V0.2 — Depth & Content

- 5th enemy family (Support/Summoner), 3+ elite affixes, 2nd boss
- Doctrines / starting civilizations (Archive unlocks wired into drafts)
- Challenge seeds + daily seed
- Balance pass from playtest telemetry (manual)

## V0.3 — World Identity

- Region/Voronoi naming, landmark quest chains, ruin histories
- More POI families (World Tree, Temporal Scar, Megasite effects on DAG)
- Minimap + world-age timeline UI

## V0.4 — Civilization Systems

- Civ traits per world (militaristic / mercantile / mystic modifiers)
- Policy choices at age transitions (pick 1 of 2 age blessings)
- Extinction events + dark ages (risk/reward anomalies)

## V0.5 — Galaxy / Deep Ascension

- Planet network view between ascensions, ascension modifiers draft
- Species evolution skins, tech distortions per ascension depth

## V1.0 — Polished Release

- Mobile touch controls, full content sweep, audio pass, achievements,
  performance hardening, full TH copy review.
